# Mastery Gates

A topic progresses to `complete` only when the learner can demonstrate the following without being led through the solution.

## Core gate

The learner can:

1. **Explain** the purpose of the method in their own words.
2. **Define** the important variables/objects and assumptions.
3. **Derive or reconstruct** the central equation/algorithm to the level expected by the module.
4. **Solve** one representative unseen problem or complete the equivalent implementation task.
5. **Interpret** the result physically/statistically and identify at least one limitation or failure mode.

## Status vocabulary

- `not-started` — no diagnostic yet
- `diagnostic` — prerequisite/mastery being assessed
- `blocked` — official topic cannot continue because a prerequisite is missing
- `bridge-active` — a narrow prerequisite bridge is in progress
- `active` — official topic currently being learned
- `practice` — concept understood but transfer/fluency is not yet secure
- `complete` — mastery gate passed
- `review` — previously completed topic due for retrieval practice

## No false completion

A correct answer obtained by following a worked template does not by itself satisfy the gate. At least one transfer question must be attempted without a step-by-step scaffold.
