## Errors and corrections

Key teaching corrections that improved the session:

- do not choose visually convenient initial centres when demonstrating random initialization;
- do not skip repeated distance calculations while the operation is still being learned;
- do not insert unexplained standardized values or other “magic numbers”;
- distinguish row-index separation from feature-space distance;
- distinguish convergence from global optimality;
- distinguish “K is supplied to the K-means algorithm” from “K can be selected using evidence outside the core iteration”;
- standardisation is conditional on feature scales/units, not universally mandatory.
